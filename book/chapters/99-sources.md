# Sources and Further Reading

Weather facts rest on agencies, archives, and published studies. Below are the organisations and papers behind the figures in this book. Records change, so check the issuing body for current values.

## Agencies and archives

- World Meteorological Organization (WMO): World Weather & Climate Extremes Archive; International Cloud Atlas (2017 edition); 2025 certification of the 829-kilometre lightning megaflash
- National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA): National Hurricane Center, National Weather Service, Storm Prediction Center, Space Weather Prediction Center, National Centers for Environmental Information, Atlantic Oceanographic and Meteorological Laboratory, Pacific Marine Environmental Laboratory
- National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA): Earth Observatory, Jet Propulsion Laboratory, and the Cassini, Juno, New Horizons, Fermi, Voyager, and Parker Solar Probe missions
- National Severe Storms Laboratory (NSSL)
- European Space Agency (ESA): MaxWave project and ASIM instrument
- Australian Bureau of Meteorology: reports on Severe Tropical Cyclone Olivia and the 2003 Canberra fires
- National Snow and Ice Data Center (NSIDC)
- Guinness World Records (for items identified as Guinness entries in the text)

## A note on accuracy

Record figures were checked against agency or news reports of the agency announcements during writing. Facts attributed to historical accounts, folklore, or single observations are labelled as such in the text. If you find an error or a newer record, the author would like to hear about it.
